2. Information We Collect

The information processed depends on the features you use, your device and the SDK and service configurations in the version of the application you install. Categories may include:

A. Account and Player Identifiers

  • A Firebase-generated player identifier associated with your game session or anonymous game account.
  • Player display name or other profile details if you choose to provide them.
  • Authentication and account-status information needed to maintain game services.

B. Gameplay and Progress Information

  • Levels started, completed or failed; scores, moves and completion times.
  • Achievements, quests, streaks, tournament or duel participation and results.
  • In-game balances and activity, such as coins, hearts, hints, XP and reward claims.
  • Game mode, settings, preferences and progress synchronization information.

C. Device, App and Diagnostic Information

  • Device model or manufacturer, Android version, app version and relevant technical configuration.
  • Crash reports, error details, performance diagnostics and app lifecycle information.
  • IP address and general network information that may be processed by Google or other service providers when their services communicate with your device.
  • Advertising identifiers or other device identifiers where permitted and used by the advertising SDK and your device settings.

D. Session and Presence Information

  • Session start and end information, session duration and app foreground/background state.
  • Online presence or last-active information used to understand whether a player connection is active.
  • Current gameplay context, such as a game mode or level, where the relevant telemetry feature is enabled.

Presence and session information is used for game operations and analytics. It should not be treated as a guaranteed real-time indication of a person's availability.

E. Advertising and Ad Interaction Information

  • Rewarded-ad requests, impressions, completions, failures and reward-placement events.
  • Ad interaction, delivery and diagnostic information processed by Google AdMob and its partners.

F. Notification Information

  • A Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token or related technical token information used to route notifications to an app installation.
  • Notification preferences and events, such as whether a notification was opened, where those features are enabled.

G. Information You Send Us

If you contact support, we receive the email address and the information you include in your message.

3. Gameplay Analytics, Events and Monitoring

Arrow Puzzle World may send gameplay and technical events to our backend so we can operate, protect and improve the game. Events may include level starts and outcomes, gameplay actions, reward claims, session lifecycle events, app errors and feature usage.

We may use aggregated or event-level information to understand activity such as daily or monthly active players, new players, session counts, level completion rates, retention, feature usage and technical reliability.

We may also process events relating to rewarded advertisements, including impressions, completions and failures. Information reported by the app about an ad or reward is not necessarily independently verified advertising revenue. We do not represent client-reported revenue estimates as confirmed revenue.

We use validation, access controls and technical safeguards intended to reduce invalid, duplicate or abusive event submissions. These measures cannot guarantee that every event is perfectly accurate.

4. How We Use Information

We may use information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate and maintain the game and its features.
  • To save and synchronize gameplay progress across supported sessions or devices.
  • To manage player profiles, scores, leaderboards, achievements, rewards and game balances.
  • To understand gameplay activity, measure feature usage and improve game design and performance.
  • To monitor sessions and connection status and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • To detect crashes, bugs, fraud, cheating, abuse and unauthorized activity.
  • To deliver and measure rewarded advertisements.
  • To send notifications where enabled and permitted by your device settings and applicable law.
  • To respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

Arrow Puzzle World uses Google AdMob to provide rewarded video advertisements. You may choose to watch an available rewarded ad to receive an in-game benefit, such as an extra heart, a hint, an opportunity to continue after losing or bonus coins.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising IDs, device and app information, IP address, ad requests, ad interactions and diagnostic information. The information processed depends on the SDK configuration, your consent choices, device settings and applicable law.

Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your location, consent status and available privacy settings. Where applicable, the app may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present consent and privacy choices.

You can review or change certain advertising and privacy controls through the consent options presented in the app and your Android device settings, where available.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google AdMob information: AdMob Help

6. Firebase Services and Local Storage

A. Firebase Authentication

The game may use Firebase Authentication, including anonymous authentication, to create or maintain a player identifier and associate game data with a player. Anonymous authentication does not necessarily require you to provide your name or email address.

B. Firebase Realtime Database

We use Firebase Realtime Database to support online game features and synchronize relevant information. Depending on the features used, this may include player profile and progress information, leaderboards, gameplay sessions, presence, telemetry events, notification tokens and related records.

C. Firebase Cloud Functions and Backend Processing

Our backend may process game actions, validate submissions, manage rewards, receive telemetry, handle notification-related events and provide authorized administrative analytics. Backend access is restricted through authentication and authorization controls intended to prevent ordinary players from accessing administrative data.

D. Firebase Cloud Messaging

Where notifications are enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging is used to deliver push notifications. The app may register an FCM token and store notification preferences or notification interaction events to support this feature.

E. Local Storage - Android Room

The app uses a local Room database to store supported game information on your device, which may include progress, preferences, session or event data awaiting synchronization. Local data may remain until it is cleared through app or Android settings, or the app is uninstalled, subject to Android backup and storage settings.

Firebase services are provided by Google. More information: Firebase Privacy and Security.

7. Push Notifications

If notification functionality is enabled, Arrow Puzzle World may send gameplay reminders, daily puzzle messages, updates or other game-related notifications.

You can control notification permission through Android settings and, where available, in-game notification preferences. Disabling notifications may prevent the app from sending some or all push notifications.

To route a notification, Firebase Cloud Messaging may process an app-instance registration token and technical delivery information. We may record notification interaction events, such as an opened notification, to measure whether notifications are useful and to improve the feature.

8. In-App Purchases and Payments

If in-app purchases are made available in the version of the game you use, payment processing is handled through the applicable Google Play billing services.

We do not ask you to send complete payment card details to us through the game. Google Play handles payment information under its own terms and privacy policy. We may receive purchase status, product or entitlement information needed to provide a purchased feature.

Google Payments information: Google Payments.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the game, maintaining player progress, operating security and analytics features, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by data type and service provider.

Local Data

Locally stored game information can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. This does not necessarily delete information already synchronized to our online services.

Online Data and Deletion Requests

If you want us to review a request to delete information associated with your player identifier, contact us at official.gamemodeon@gmail.com. Include your player ID if you can access it, but do not send your password or other authentication secrets.

We may need to verify and clarify a request before processing it. We will review valid requests and handle them within a reasonable period, subject to applicable law, legitimate operational needs and information we are legally required or permitted to retain. Deleting local app data or uninstalling the game does not by itself guarantee deletion of server-side records.

Information processed independently by Google or another provider may also be subject to that provider's retention and deletion practices.
